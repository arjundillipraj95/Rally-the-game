## Phases at a glance

Lag comes first because every new mechanic is timing-based (wind-ups, perfect jousts, dodging). Animation work starts in parallel from day one, because models take time to source.

| Phase | Goal | Playable at the end | Main models | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Lag fix and cleanup | Today's game, smoother for far-away players | O, S | — |
| 1 | Foundation: roles, ability system, economy, morale, healing | Pick a role; stats, places, upgrades and respawn work | S, O | 0 |
| 2 | Captain kits and horse rules | Each role fights with its own basic attack and two abilities | S | 1 |
| 3 | Captain ults | Cut down, long shot and joust, with wind-ups | S, O | 2 |
| 4 | Armies: role recruits, new soldiers, upgrades, siege | Spearmen, swordsmen, cavalry and stone throwers in battle | S, O | 1 |
| 5 | Army ults | Testudo march, arrow storm and the wedge | O | 4 |
| 6 | Screens: controls and lobby | The mocked-up battle screen and role-pick lobby | S, H | 2, 3, 5 |
| 7 | Bots | Computer captains that play each role well | O | 2–5 |
| 8 | Animation, models and feel | Real animations, effects and sound for every move | O, S | Arjun's GLBs; runs alongside 1–7 |
| 9 | Balance, playtest, launch | Tuned numbers, friends' playtest, switch on for everyone | O, S | All |

Phases 2–3 and 4–5 can run side by side once Phase 1 is done.
