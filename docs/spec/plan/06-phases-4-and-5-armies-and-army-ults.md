## Phases 4 and 5: armies and army ults

Goal: each role leads its own army (spearmen, archers, swordsmen, cavalry, stone throwers) with role upgrades, and each army has its ult. This is the largest phase; cavalry, the stone thrower and formations are the hardest work in the whole plan.
