## Army ults

Each captain fills his own morale bar and fires his own army ult by **dragging from the ****army-ult button (its gold ring is the morale bar)**** and dropping on the target** (a preview shows the result; dragging back cancels). Teammates can time theirs together, but nothing forces it. Today's rally horn becomes this.

|  | Testudo march (Defender) | Arrow storm (Attacker) | The wedge (Raider) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feel | A grind | Fire from the sky | A crash |
| What happens | Shields lock with a roof overhead and **everyone heals 25%**; then the block marches toward the drop: **push, push, spear** (about 1.5 s per cycle, 4 cycles over 6 s, about 12 m of ground gained), to a war drum and shouts. Arrow-proof while marching. A captain tapping Attack on the spear beat gets the rhythm bonus | Every archer fires at one 10 m area for 4 s; fire arrows set the ground burning; stone throwers fire too | Riders form a wedge behind the captain (about 1 s, the warning), then charge for about 3 s along the dragged path. **Braces don't stop it**; riders take a quarter of the usual horse damage; every soldier hit loses formation for 3 s. The wedge comes out behind the line and each rider's next attack is boosted with a bonus from behind; swordsmen sprint in after |
| Counterplay | Faces forward: flanks and back are open; stones (half damage) and fire pots land on it; arrows do nothing; a basic attack can't break it | A 2 s warning marker | Read the form-up and get away; a testudo march head-on stops it |

Healing lives in the Defender's kit (the testudo and the cut down) and in held places, nowhere else.

**Hit rules for army ults:** arrows do no damage to a testudo march and stones do half; melee hits do normal damage; every unit the wedge hits, and the wedge itself, takes normal damage from arrows, stones and melee. A testudo march and a wedge meeting head-on stop each other. Nothing interrupts an ult once it starts, neither a basic attack nor an ability. The only answers are getting away from it during its wind-up, or another ult. Every ult therefore has a visible wind-up.
