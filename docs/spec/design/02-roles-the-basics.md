## Roles: the basics

Each player picks a role and a lane plan in the lobby; the role is locked for the match.

| Decision | Agreed |
|---|---|
| Names | **Defender** (tank), **Attacker** (damage), **Raider** (jungler / speedster) |
| When | In the free-play lobby, 30 s (at least 10 s after the last player joins); a 5 s countdown once everyone has locked in. The warm-up fight keeps people busy while they wait |
| Two picks | **Role** (locked for the match) and **lane plan**: left, right or jungle (a plan only, not a rule) |
| Talking it through | Live picks visible to the team, one-tap quick phrases ("I'll Defend", "Need a Raider"), the plan shown on the side map in the lobby only, a gentle "no Defender on your team" nudge |
| Enemies | See your team's picks only when the battle starts |
| Duplicates | Allowed, including three of one role on a team |
| No pick | The role you last played |
| Switching | None. Locked for the whole match |
| Cost | Free |
