## Economy and pricing

Gold must be scarce enough that upgrades are real choices; today it isn't (about 6,000–8,000 per captain per match against about 2,000 of things to buy), so base income drops from 6 to 3 per second.

**Where a captain's gold comes from today** (mountain Crown, 10 minutes): starting gold 150; base income 6/s (about 3,600); places held, castle 2/s and village 1/s to every army on the side; taking a place, castle 200 and village 100 (plus a share of the owner's gold); kills, 8 a soldier and 25 a captain; camps 50; war chest 150.

**Rules for the numbers pass:**

- Captain upgrade 1 must compete with the first recruits: starting gold must not buy it outright in the first second.
- Attackers and their soldiers count 1.5 blows on the war chest; a stone counts 6.
- Run bot test matches, measure gold at 2:00, 5:00, 8:00 and 10:00 and what each role buys when, then set every price; check snowballing (the side behind should still get its ults in time).

**Buying:** everything is bought from the upgrade menu, anywhere on the map, as today; the menu will be made better. There is no levelling in a match: the captain progresses only through gold, the Age of Empires way.
