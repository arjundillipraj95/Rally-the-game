## Business plan

Rally makes money the PUBG way: looks and a season pass, never gameplay. There are no factions; cultural styles (Roman, Chola, Mongol, Viking) are skins in the shop.

- **What we sell:** army and captain skin sets, the Gold season track, horse and captain looks, army-ult animations and victory poses, banners and titles; later, private battles for clans and tournaments.
- **Rally's edge:** a skin dresses your captain and your whole army, so it's seen across the battlefield.
- **Why it scales:** a link is the whole install (cheap growth); each new culture is a season of cosmetics, not balance work; selling on the web avoids the 30% app-store cut; players host battles, so servers stay cheap.
- **Order:** fix lag, build roles, prove retention on the dashboard, then sell skins and the pass.
