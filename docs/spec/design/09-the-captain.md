## The captain

The captain fights like a Unite or League character (tap attack with a boosted hit, two abilities, an ult) and progresses only with gold.

**The kit**

|  | Defender | Attacker | Raider |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base weapon | Spear | Bow | Sword |
| Basic | Tap, tap, **shield bash** (staggers) | Arrows at the nearest unit; the third is **2 quick arrows** | Tap, tap, **cleaving slash** |
| Ability 1 | **Shield retreat:** blocks everything from the front, arrows included; covers allies directly behind | **Piercing shot:** aimed, goes through several, boosted damage, slows; stops a galloping horse | **Charge** on horseback (a short leap when he has no horse) |
| Ability 2 | **Spear sweep:** area damage, knockback and stagger; heavy horse damage, stops a charge | **Evade:** a dash to escape | **Spin:** 2–3 s of area damage on foot, moving in or out; pressed while mounted, he jumps down into it |
| Ult | **The cut down:** a huge overhead strike, area damage on impact, heals 50% of the damage dealt (capped at 40% of his health) | **The long shot** (like Decidueye's): plants and draws about 1 s with an aim line, then one arrow about 60 m; passes through soldiers, stops at the first captain; more damage the more wounded he is | **The joust**, horseback only: locks on a captain within about 25 m; mounted target is unhorsed for certain; on foot below 35% health, a kill; otherwise a big hit and knockdown. Without a horse the charge is kept until he remounts |

The rhythm bonus stays: the third hit is always boosted, and stronger still if timed on the beat. Abilities and ults: **tap auto-aims, drag aims manually.** Ults are on a cooldown (about 90 s).

The long shot on a mounted target hits the horse first (the usual rule); a hit that kills the horse unhorses the rider, which is the Attacker's long-range answer to a Raider. A joust can only be avoided by getting away (out of its lock-on range, or behind your own soldiers) or answered with an ult; it shows a lock-on marker on the target for about 1 s first.

**Captain upgrades (gold)**

|  | Unlocks | Buff | Aura | Respawn after | Target time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | Basic attack only | — | — | 5 s | — |
| Upgrade 1 | Abilities 1 and 2 | +10% health, +8% damage | Level 1 | 7 s | About 1:00 |
| Upgrade 2 | The ult | +10% health, +8% damage | Level 2 | 9 s | About 4:00, before the war chest |
| Upgrade 3 | — | +10% health, +8% damage | Level 3 | 10 s | About 7:30, before the Crown |

Longer respawn is the trade-off for power, and a side that's behind respawns faster (a natural catch-up). Home is about 110 m from the glacier, so a Crown death really costs about 20 s. While dead, the shop stays open and the camera can move.

**Second weapon:** one purchase per match, any of the pool except your base weapon. It changes only your basic attack; abilities stay your role's. The weapon-swap button switches between the two.

| Weapon | Its job |
|---|---|
| Sword | Fast combo, balanced |
| Spear | Reach, hits two in a line |
| Javelins | Short-range burst; a full draw unhorses |
| Bow | Long range, steady damage; never at a gallop |
| Axe | Slow, wide, cleaves 3; each hit breaks a soldier's formation (shield, brace, Hold) for 2 s; never breaks a captain's ability or an army ult's formation |

**Shared rules:** execute a captain below 15% health (alone, not mid-ability); a 1 s finisher in which only the target is locked, so a teammate can save him by hitting the executor. Captain against captain is decided by skill and timing.

**Removed from today:** the block/parry button, evades (pounce, sidestep, backstep), the streak whirlwind, jump, and the Aura, Horse and Armour upgrades. Jousting stays for the Raider; duels may stay as a challenge mode.
