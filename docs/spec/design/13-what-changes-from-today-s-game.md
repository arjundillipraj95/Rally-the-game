## What changes from today's game

| Area | Today | New design |
|---|---|---|
| Captains | All the same: 150 health, 22 damage | Three roles with different stats, kits and armies |
| Army | 5 places for everyone; 3 footmen and 2 archers | 7 / 5 / 3 by role; recruits limited by role; new swordsmen, cavalry and stone thrower |
| Holding places | One pot split evenly across the side | Flat per captain, by role |
| Weapons | Free switch between sword, spear and javelins | A base weapon by role plus one bought second weapon from five (adds bow and axe) |
| Defence | Block and parry button, evades | Removed; defence comes from abilities |
| Big moments | Streak whirlwind, rally horn | Removed; ults and army ults instead |
| Captain upgrades | Aura, Horse, Armour; captain follows the footman tiers | Three captain upgrades: abilities, ult, final buff, each with an aura level |
| Horses | Footmen unhorse riders; a new horse on a 20 s cooldown anywhere | Only a perfect hit unhorses; the horse takes hits first; new horses come from held places |
| Healing | Home pocket only (on the mountain) | Also castles, the keep and villages you hold, in pulses |
| Respawn | 5 s for players, flat | 5 / 7 / 9 / 10 s by captain upgrade |
| Gold | 6 per second base income | 3 per second; prices to be set by test runs |
