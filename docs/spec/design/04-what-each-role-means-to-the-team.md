## What each role means to the team

Each role has a job through the match, an aura for allies nearby, and its own way of filling morale. Points never change by role.

**The job through a match**

| Time | Defender | Attacker | Raider |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–5:00, land grab | Takes the nearest castle and holds it | Pushes a lane, breaks the first gate | Clears camps, takes villages, scouts |
| 5:00, war chest | Holds the ground around the chest | Leads the hits on the chest | Hunts enemy Attackers going for it |
| 8:00, the Crown | Holds the glacier approach | Breaks the glacier | Cuts off enemy Attackers, picks off archers |

**Auras** apply to every ally in range: teammates, their captains and their soldiers. Only one aura of each kind applies (the strongest), so stacking three Defenders doesn't stack the effect. Auras grow with captain upgrades (see The captain).

|  | Aura | Level 1 / 2 / 3 | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defender | Allies take less damage | 5% / 8% / 12% | 12 / 15 / 18 m |
| Attacker | Allies deal more damage | 5% / 8% / 12% | 12 / 15 / 18 m |
| Raider | Allies move faster | 5% / 8% / 12% | 12 / 15 / 18 m |

**Morale** is one bar per captain, the same bar as today's army morale. It fills from what your men see you do (they must be within about 20 m). At 100 the army ult is ready; firing it drops the bar to 50; below 20 the army routs, as today.

| Moment | Morale |
|---|---|
| Win a duel (you beat a captain one on one) | +30 |
| Kill an enemy captain in front of your men | +20 |
| Kill combo: 2 or more kills within 5 s | +10 per extra kill |
| Execute a captain | +15 |
| Lead from the front: hits landed with at least 3 of your men close by | +1 per hit |
| Win the fight: an enemy army routs near you | +20 |
| Your men watch you fall | −35 |
| Each of your men dies | −3 |

**Ult charge** is separate from morale: doing your role's job brings your captain ult back faster.

| Role | Ult cooldown shortens by |
|---|---|
| Defender | Runs 1.5× faster while inside a place you hold with enemies within about 20 m (defending, not hiding) |
| Attacker | −15 s per captain kill, −1 s per soldier kill, −1 s per gate, tower or war chest hit |
| Raider | −15 s per camp cleared, −10 s per village taken, −10 s per captain kill |

These replace the earlier per-role map bonuses. The one that stays is the Attacker's +50% damage to structures (1.5 blows on the war chest). Exact numbers are set in the numbers pass.
