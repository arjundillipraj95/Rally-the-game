## The triangle and stat profiles

**Defender beats Raider, Raider beats Attacker, Attacker beats Defender**, as an edge of about 60/40, not a hard counter. The roles line up with the soldiers: spears stop horses, horses run down archers, archers wear down footmen.

| Matchup | Why, for captains | Why, for armies | The underdog's way to win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defender beats Raider | Shield and sweep stop a charge; a fragile Raider can't burst a tank | Braced spears stop cavalry | Raider: flank, catch spearmen moving, bait the sweep, outscore on the far side of the map |
| Raider beats Attacker | Reaches the backline; the Attacker has only his evade | Cavalry run down archers | Attacker: the piercing shot stops a gallop, the long shot unhorses, stay near his own army |
| Attacker beats Defender | Range and damage wear down a slow tank | Archers shred footmen; stones break formations | Defender: shield retreat, close the distance, Marksman archers outrange the Attacker's, the testudo march |

**Starting stat profiles** (against today's captain: 150 health, 22 damage). The role applies to the captain and his whole army.

|  | Defender | Attacker | Raider |
|---|---|---|---|
| Captain health | +35% (about 200) | −20% (120) | Normal (150) |
| Captain damage | −20% | +25% | Normal |
| Captain speed | −5% | Normal | +15%, horse +15% |
| Soldiers' health | +20% | −10% | Normal |
| Soldiers' damage | −10% | +15% | Normal |
| Soldiers' speed | Normal | Normal | +10% |

Speed costs the Raider army size (see Armies). Testing will track win rate per role pairing and per team composition, aiming for 40–60%.
