## Horses and cavalry

Every captain has a horse; only the Raider fights from the saddle with a mounted ability, and only a perfect hit knocks a rider off.

- **Two health bars:** every hit goes to the horse first, then the rider (the rider has the lower health). The same for captains and cavalry soldiers.
- **Unhorsing:** only a perfect lance joust or a perfect brace. Otherwise spears stop the charge and hurt the horse, and the rider fights on.
- **Bracing depends on the order:**

| Spearmen are… | Horse damage | The charge |
|---|---|---|
| On Hold (the captain's order is the skill) | 20 + 5 × horse speed: gallop about 85, trot 60, walk 35 | Stops |
| Standing, facing the horse | Half of that | Slowed by half, gets through |
| Moving or following | A normal hit | Gets through and bowls them over |

- **Arrows against horses** (accuracy drops with speed, no damage reduction):

| Horse is… | Archer | Marksman |
|---|---|---|
| Standing | Every arrow | Every arrow |
| Trotting | 2 in 3 | 9 in 10 |
| Galloping | 1 in 3 | 2 in 3 |

Barding pushes a galloping horse's hit chance down by 10 points. The same rule applies to watchtowers and gate towers. A javelin at close range hits a galloping horse 1 in 2.

- **Captains on horseback:** everyone can make basic attacks from the saddle; the horse is extra life and an escape. Only the Raider has a mounted ability (the charge) and jousting. A bow tap halts the horse and shoots after a short draw (about 0.4 s); riding on restarts the gallop from slow.
- **Losing a horse:** after 30 s, a new horse sets off from the nearest place your side holds and runs to you; it can be intercepted and killed on the way (the wait restarts). Respawning gives a full horse. A Raider's stable (Fortify) sends horses faster.
- **Raider cavalry:** mounting a swordsman costs 50 gold and 1 more place; the horse goes to the swordsman with the most health. When the horse dies he fights on as a swordsman (back to 1 place) and can be remounted for 50 at a held place.
