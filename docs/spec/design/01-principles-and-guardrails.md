## Principles and guardrails

- **Classes, not champions.** Three roles, forever. Your captain is you (wardrobe, crest), not a named hero.
- **The army is the main character.** Most of a role's power is in the army it leads, not the captain's kit.
- **One kit per role.** Two abilities and one ult per role; everything else is shared. No faction, skin or purchase ever changes a captain's moves.
- **No magic.** Every effect is something a real army could do: training, formation, morale, fire, terrain, a well-timed order. Heroic exaggeration is fine.
- **No role loses by design.** The triangle sets the edge in a straight fight (about 60/40); strategy must give the underdog a real way to win, in solo especially. Every change is checked against the three solo matchups.
- **Gameplay is never for sale.** Money comes from looks and the season pass, as in PUBG.
- **Discuss, agree, then build.** Nothing is built until Arjun says go.

Everyone starts **equal in power, different in role**: the same gold, the same chances, a different job.
