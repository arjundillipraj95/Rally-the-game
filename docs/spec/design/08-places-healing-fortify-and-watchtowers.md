## Places: healing, fortify and watchtowers

Holding a place heals your side in pulses, and fortifications and watchtowers differ by the role that builds them.

**Healing** (captains and soldiers, in pulses you can see):

| Where | Heal | When |
|---|---|---|
| Home pocket | 18 per second (as today) | Always |
| Castle or keep you hold | +8 every 3 s | Even under fire from outside; none while enemies are inside (contested) |
| Village you hold | +4 every 3 s | The same |

One archer against one healing defender roughly breaks even; a real Attacker army still breaks through.

**Fortify by role:**

| Fortified by | Gates | Gate towers | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defender | Tougher (×3 at full) | Normal, fewer shots | Mends faster |
| Attacker | Normal | More towers, faster fire | — |
| Raider | Normal | Normal | A stable: horses come back faster here; sees further from the castle |

**Watchtowers by role:**

| Built by | Arrows per volley | Health | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defender | 3 | Double | Slowly repairs itself while he holds the place |
| Attacker | 5 | Normal | The damage tower |
| Raider | 3 | Normal | Sees twice as far through fog and night |

Neither Fortify nor watchtowers lock out the Raider: tower arrows mostly miss galloping horses.
