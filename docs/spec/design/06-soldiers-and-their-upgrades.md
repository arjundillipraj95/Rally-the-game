## Soldiers and their upgrades

Every unit has a base version and two upgrades: **upgrade 1 adds a move plus a small stat bump, upgrade 2 is the big buff**, and every upgrade changes how the soldier looks.

| Role | Unit | Base | Upgrade 1 (move) | Upgrade 2 (buff) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Defender | Spearman | Spear and big shield; braces on Hold | **Pike & javelin:** a javelin every 8 s at any enemy within 10 m (charging or running past) unless in melee; longer reach, +25% brace damage; +10 health, +2 damage. Looks: longer pike, javelins on the back | **Heavy armour:** +35% health, +15% damage, better block, slightly slower. Looks: plate, closed helmet, rimmed shield |
| Defender | Archer | Standard bow, 22 m | **Volley from the walls:** two arrows at once inside a place you hold | **Marksman:** 29 m, tighter aim, more health and damage; hits a galloping horse 2 in 3 |
| Attacker | Archer | Standard bow, 22 m | **Fire arrows:** ×3 against gates, towers and throwers, a small burn on soldiers. Looks: burning heads, fire pot | **Longbow:** 26 m, +20% damage, more health; hits a galloping horse 1 in 2. Looks: tall bow, hood |
| Attacker | Stone thrower (5 places) | 300 health, 40 m, reload 6 s, 3.5 m area (45 centre to 15 edge), ×4 against gates, can't hit within 10 m, slow; misses movers, punishes standing lines and shieldwalls; 6 blows on the war chest | **Fire pots:** every third shot burns the ground for 5 s (forces a spear line to move, so it can't brace); reload 5.5 s | **Reinforced:** +50% health, range 50 m, moves faster |
| Raider | Swordsman | Sword and small shield, fast | **Pursuit:** first hit on a running enemy slows him 2 s; hits from behind ×1.5 | **Mail:** +30% health, +15% damage, better block |
| Raider | Cavalry (a mounted swordsman) | Horse with 120 health; the charge bowls men over | **Lances:** a lance charge, +40% charge damage; the lance returns 10 s after a charge; +10 horse health | **Barding:** horse health +50%; a galloping horse is hit 10 points less often |

**Soldiers follow the Hold order when bracing** (see Horses and cavalry). Soldiers' behaviour in each situation is still to design.

**Stone thrower against gates:** 45 × 4 = 180 per stone, so a castle gate (700) falls in about 4 stones and a small gate (450) in 3. Its area damage is meant to counter a big Defender army: a stone landing in a crowd hits many men. **Javelins and arrows**: every hit shows a visible throw, a thud and spark, the damage number, and a stagger on a hard-hit horse, so the spear line's effect is felt.

**Soldiers by order** (agreed). Follow: your army does what you do. Hold: spears brace, others hold position. Charge: attack; archers shoot the nearest.

| Unit | Follow | Hold | Charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spearman | Loose formation near the captain | Line facing the nearest threat, braces; javelin as they close | Advances, no brace |
| Archer | About 8 m behind the front, shoots the nearest | Stays put and shoots | Moves to range, shoots the nearest |
| Swordsman | Loose around the captain | Guards the spot | Hunts archers and throwers from the flank |
| Cavalry | At the captain's flank | Stays mounted, no charging | Charges, breaks off, wheels, charges again; avoids braced lines |
| Stone thrower | Behind the army | Fires at the biggest crowd or the nearest gate | Same as Hold |

By situation: attacking a castle, throwers and fire arrows hit the gate while spearmen cover the throwers and cavalry circle for archers outside, and Charge sends everyone in once the gate breaks; defending, archers man the walls, spearmen brace at the gate and cavalry sally out against throwers; in the night arena arrows are blocked, so archers hold and melee closes; soldiers hit the war chest only when no enemy is near; at the Crown soldiers hold the ring and keep enemy captains off (they can't hit the glacier).

**Wind-ups** (agreed): 1.2 s for the joust and the long shot, 1 s for the cut down and the wedge, a 2 s marker for the arrow storm. **Two perfect lances at once** (agreed): both riders are rocked and ride past. Archers step back a few metres when an enemy reaches them (agreed); soldiers don't dodge ult wind-ups by themselves, that is the captain's command. Duels (agreed): equal terms, both on foot at 150 health with a melee weapon, abilities and ults locked; Block returns only in duels with today's directional reads and parry (the Ability 1 button becomes Block); a Challenge button appears near an enemy captain within 10 m; today's ring, timer and morale rules stay. **Roles apply to the Crown only at first**; other modes keep today's rules.
