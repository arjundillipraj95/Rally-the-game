# F17c The spear: upright when held, poke / sweep / lunge, braced level; and the gear you see now follows it

Model: Opus · Written and built 2026-11-13 in Arjun's animation chat ("while holding the spear it needs to be upright,
while fighting it needs to hit forward; hit one poke, hit 2 sweep, hit 3 stronger thrust"; "Yes please fix")
Status: built, tests below. On `roles`.

## What was wrong
- **The spear you see never moved (F17b's aim included).** Since F1b a man's plain gear near the camera is folded into
  one skinned mesh, each piece pinned to its nearest bone or "mover"; the originals are hidden (layer 31). The spear's
  pieces were pinned to the hand as they sat at build time, so F17b aimed the hidden spear. Measured: hiding it changed
  nothing in the picture.
- Held, the spear came out level at chest height (the one-handed guard clip), and braced it lay across his body.
- The rules send a spear's three presses as swingKind 3, 3, 5 (thrust, thrust, the skewer lunge); the clips were picked
  as if it were the sword's 0, 1, 4, so the second press never drew anything different.

## What was built
1. `render/heroes.js`: the folded gear also hangs on the spear's slide joint (`foldGear(R, [shield, cape, R.slide])`).
2. `render/rig/cliprig.js`: a slide joint inside the spear's pivot (`R.slide`) and its butt (`R.butt`), so the shaft
   slides along its length in his grip.
3. `render/heroes.js` `aimShaft`, by state (`ANIM.shaftAim`), eased in the world, not in his fast-swinging hand:
   upright, tipped 10 degrees forward, slid down to rest on its butt (standing, walking, running; the idle clip head to
   toe, no `spearIdle`); level along his facing in a poke, the lunge and when braced; the sweep an arc from his right
   across his front, dead ahead as the blow lands (`sweepArc`); mounted, a lance couched.
4. `config.js`: the second spear press (swingKind 3, combo 1) is drawn as the sweep (`ANIM.spearSweep`); the skewer
   (swingKind 5) as `spearLunge` (the thrust clip, wound up longer, legs in it, `ANIM.clipOf`).
5. `render/soldiers.js`: the sweep leaves a ribbon from the spearhead; the poke and lunge streak starts at the spearhead.
