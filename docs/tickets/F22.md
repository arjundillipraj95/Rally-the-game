# F22 Players keep their names on every phone

Model: O · Written 14 Nov from Arjun's report (Ana saw him as "Sir Mud") and his "go"
Status: built (not shipped)

## What to build
- A joined phone keeps every player the host lists for as long as the host is talking to it. Before, the host
  only re-sent its list when something changed, so after 6 s in battle the other joined players dropped off
  and showed as a bare colour ("Blue third captain") or a computer captain's name.
- A joined phone follows the host's seats all battle, not just at the start: a player seated after the start
  counts as a player (named), and one who leaves goes back to the computer.

## Done when
`game/store/r_F22.mjs`: three friends in one Trio party; after 12 s every phone names the other two and counts
them as players (12 checks); a seat taken away and given back on the host shows on a joined phone (2 checks).

## Where to start in the code
`game/src/net/p2p.js` (`relayed`, joined phone's host handler), `game/src/net/netgame.js` (`seatHumans`).

## Found on the way (not fixed here)
- If a joined phone hears nothing from the host for 7.5 s (host phone frozen or in the background), it takes
  over as a new host, but the old host carries on alone: the battle splits in two, and on the new host's phone
  the old host is a computer captain with a computer name. This may also explain Ana's "Sir Mud".
- `store/latejoin.mjs` no longer runs (no Join button after F2's lobby); joining a running battle can't be
  tested headless as it stands.
