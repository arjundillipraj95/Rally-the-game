# F6 + F5 Hit feel and the death sequence

Model: Fable 5.2 · Written by the builder 11 Nov 2026 from Arjun's brief (owner's queue entry #2), design rev as mirrored in `docs/spec/` on that day
Status: built (not shipped). On `roles`, waiting for Arjun's "ship".

## What to build
Fights must feel like Pokemon Unite: every hit, block and death is felt, readable and smooth on a mid-range
phone. Friends' playtest: hits and blocks aren't felt ("when 2 units attack there is no reality to it"), and
dying "is very random".

**F6 Hit feel**, in Arjun's order:
1. Captain against captain, both swinging into each other: the blades meet. Spark burst, metal clang, a short
   hit-stop, both pushed back a step, a small camera kick.
2. A blocked hit: shield thud, the shield flashes, the attacker's weapon rebounds, the defender slides back a
   little, a smaller camera nudge if it involves you.
3. A captain cutting down a soldier: a weighty slash, hit-stop, the soldier knocked back and falling properly.
   Blood-free: dust, stars, a comic yelp.
Then the rest at lower intensity (soldier against soldier, a captain hit by a soldier, arrows, horses), hit
reactions for everyone, and a little more for your own hits, with a short vibration on Android.

**F5 Death sequence** (Arjun agreed): your captain falls, the screen greys, a card shows who killed you (name,
colour, what with) and a respawn countdown, then a fade to black and back in at home. His men carry on with
their order. Other captains: a clear fall and a kill-feed line. Soldiers: fall, lie, fade; never vanish mid-air.

Everything shows the same on joined phones. Numbers live in `game/src/config.js` (`FEEL`).

## Test results (11 Nov, headless, Low graphics)
- `node store/r_F6.mjs`: 49/49. Clash, block and cut each set off hit-stop, flash and sound on the host and on the
  joined phone, with either phone's captain in it; pushes measured. Death on both phones: grey, the right killer's
  name and colour, countdown 4 3 2 1, respawn; camera at most 0.9 m a frame in view, the move home under full
  black. 12 men taken away on each phone, none sooner than 14 s after falling, none standing or in the air.
- Frame cost, Low, Crown start: draw calls 1218 before, 1191 now (-2.2%); triangles 547,972 before, 542,711 now (-1.0%).
- `crown2sim` roles off and on: both to the end of the match, no errors. `crown2online` roles off and on: both
  phones in one battle, same state, no errors (the test now takes `Q`, as crown2sim does).
- Lag (250 ms, 5% loss, 60 s), before -> after: response 133 -> 128 ms; position error 1.15 -> 0.90 m mean;
  jumps over 0.5 m 167 -> 55 (run-to-run noise is large; not worse).

## What was built
| Moment | The rules (host) | Every phone plays |
|---|---|---|
| Blades meet (`fxclash`) | Two captains on foot, each with a plain blow on its way at the other, face to face: neither lands, both are pushed back (about 1 m each) and held 0.22 s. Heavy blows, bashes, ripostes and the rampage go through | White star flash and sparks between them, sword-on-sword ring, both bodies hold 80 ms, both reel back. Yours: the whole screen holds 75 ms, camera kick, "CLASH!", vibration |
| On a shield (`fxblk`, when a captain is in it) | The defender now keeps 55% of the push (was 40%) | A ring flash on the shield, a deep thud, the attacker rocks back off it, both hold 45 ms. Yours: camera nudge, vibration |
| Cut down (`fxcut`, a captain kills a soldier) | As before (he is thrown back; big hits launch him) | A wide gold stroke, yellow stars and dust, a low whoosh and a yelp, 90 ms hold, then he falls away from the blade (joined phones too: before, the direction there was random) |
| Your own blows | Same hit-stop and shake as before, now from `FEEL.own`; plus a short vibration when one lands | |
| Any blow that lands | | The struck man holds his pose 25-60 ms by how hard it was (`FEEL.light`), near the camera only |
| Your death | `fell` now carries who and how; every captain's fall is a `capDown` line | Camera stays on your body (pulled back a little), grey screen, card, countdown, black, cut to home, back in |

## Decisions I made (Arjun said go; tell me if any is wrong)
1. **A clash cancels both blows.** Before, two captains swinging together both took the hit. Now neither does.
   This changes captain duels a little (fewer trades). Tune or switch off with `FEEL.clash.win` (0 = never).
2. **"Never more than 2 m in one frame" is measured on frames you can see.** The move home on respawn is one cut
   under full black (measured 20 m for the host, 110-121 m for the joiner), not a glide. That is what a fade is.
3. **On Low graphics the grey is a veil over the picture**, not a filter on it (no extra work for a weak phone).
   Medium and High desaturate the picture itself. Only the veil has been seen (the tests run on Low).
4. **Every captain cut down by an enemy is now a feed line on the mountain**, whoever's side (one who fell to a
   garrison or the chasm: only your own side's). Before, the mountain's feed showed
   only your own side's news. The feed still shows at most one line every 5 s there.
5. **The "You fell!" banner is gone**: the card says it.
6. **Vibration**: a new setting (Settings, Vibration, on by default; `rally-vibe`). There was none before.
7. **The attacker's "rebound" off a shield is how he is drawn** (he rocks back), not a rule: nobody loses a turn.
8. **Shield moments are sent to joined phones only when a captain is in them.** Soldier against soldier stays as it
   was (joined phones work those out from health dropping), so the big moments stand out and snapshots stay small.
9. **Fixed while here:** a fallen body hopped about 10 cm every half second for as long as it lay (fall speed kept
   building on the ground). It now comes to rest. Joined phones took bodies away after 12 s, the host after 14:
   both 14 now (`FEEL.death.body`), and never while a body is in the air.
10. With no way back (no respawns left), the camera stays on your body 4 s, then cuts under black to an ally.

## Sounds needed (placeholders are in `ui/audio.js`, built from the files we have plus synthesis)
1. `clash`: two swords meeting edge to edge, a bright strike and a short ring (3 or 4 takes).
2. `shield`: a blow stopped dead on a wooden shield, a deep thud (3 or 4 takes).
3. `cut`: a heavy sword cut through the air and landing, low and wide (2 or 3 takes).
4. `yelp`: a short comic yelp or "oof!" for a soldier going down (4 to 6 takes, different voices).

## Not verified
- **The look and feel on a real phone.** Everything was run in a headless browser with software drawing. The
  counters prove each effect is set off; they don't prove it feels right. Arjun judges on his phone.
- Vibration on a real Android (the test counts the pulses asked for).
- The grey filter on Medium and High; the card on a small portrait screen.
- 60 fps on a mid-range phone: only draw calls and triangles were compared.
- Duo and Trio (the test battle is Solo, four captains).
- `?roles=1`: no code reads it yet, so "roles on" and "roles off" are the same game today.

## Found, not fixed (owner: tickets or STATE entries)
- The host's own hit-stop slows the rules for everybody: the whole battle runs at 6% speed for 30-100 ms each
  time the host's captain lands a blow. It was so before F6; F6 adds the clash to it. Joined phones' own hit-stop
  is theirs alone. Suggest: hold only the picture on the host.
- A joiner who takes over a computer captain can start on its horse.
- Far-off dead soldiers are drawn as the old box figure (the far figure has no fallen pose).
