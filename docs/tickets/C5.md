## What to build
Page `src/champions/sigrun.js` (under 300 lines) on the C1 framework. Every move must look like what it does; Unite feel
(big readable shapes, a clear area on the ground, a bright impact, a lingering trace); hits felt by both sides with
`impact()` (render/feel.js), the wolf reacting (flinch, yelp, snarl on heavy hits) as the horse does (FEEL.horse).
Every move checked on the wolf and on foot.

  has raided from Birka to Lindisfarne since. The skalds say whoever wears the Crown is never forgotten. She doesn't want a
  kingdom. She wants a saga." Health 300; 27 a hit at 1.65/s (44.6/s); reach 1.3 (axe), 1.6 riding (wolf); 6.8 on foot.

  Recruits swordsmen and cavalry (Raider: 3 places, +3 a castle). Her men take her shares: health -25%, damage -10%,
  rate +18%, speed +10%. Her swordsmen carry Viking axes and round shields: Drengr (Pursuit), Huskarl (Mail), Úlfheðnar

  On the wolf: pounce, clamp on the neck, one hard shake, the target drops. On foot: leaping overhead chop, the target

## Design rules it relies on
Rulebook: one job per ability, area shown first, wind-up 0.3 s or less, never locked over 0.5 s (ults aside), no stun
over 1 s, a full combo at most 50% of a champion in 2 s; level path (sp1 3, sp2 5, ults 7), +5% health and damage a level;
horse rules for every champion (17 Nov); Raider: Defender beats Raider (braces stop her spin charge), Raider beats Attacker.
