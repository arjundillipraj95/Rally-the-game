# F14 Adapts to any phone

Model: O · Built 2026-11-13 by Opus (Arjun: "you start the build"; Group A of the visual polish pass in the design doc)
Status: built (not shipped)

## What to build
Graphics on Auto keep adjusting the whole battle, not just the first 8 s: down when slow, back up when
smooth, below Low a render-scale step (down to 0.7), never above the detected level, and the level
reached is kept for the next battle on that phone. Targets: 60 fps iPhone 13+ / Galaxy S23+, 30+ on
any Android. Never cut on any tier: hit flash, team colours, the Crown beam (none are tied to quality).

## Done when
`store/r_F14.mjs` (the rule fed frame rates, no browser) and `store/r_F14b.mjs` (a slow headless
battle steps the render scale down; stalls don't count) pass.

## How it works
- Numbers: `ADAPT` in `config.js` (window 4 s, settle 2 s, down < 28 fps, up > 55 fps for 3 windows,
  60 s ban on climbing back to a level it just fell from, scale step 0.15, floor 0.7).
- Rule: `adaptStep` in `render/quality.js` (pure). Loop: `watchdog` in `main.js`.
- Live changes: shadows, pixel ratio x scale, particle caps. Grass count and antialiasing follow the
  level at the next battle / reload (as before).
- Kept: `localStorage['rally-quality-auto']`. A manual Low/Medium/High setting turns all of this off.

## Out of scope
Groups B to D of the polish pass (ambient particles, wind sway, light shafts, art pass).
