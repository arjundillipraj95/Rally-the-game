# F14 Adapts to any phone

Model: S · First built 13 Nov by Opus (level-then-scale rule); rebuilt the same day to Arjun's picks (his "go" to replace it)
Status: built (not shipped). r_F14 37/37, r_F14b 9/9, crown2sim + crown2online pass roles off and on. Wedge not reproduced (see below)

## What to build (Arjun's picks, 13 Nov)
- First guess from the graphics chip and screen size; measure frame rate for the first 5 s; then adjust.
- Lower in this order: render resolution, shadows, clouds and far scenery, detail on distant soldiers.
  Raise again after the phone has stayed smooth for a while.
- Keep watching after the start (phones slow down when hot). Mid-fight only resolution changes; the visible
  changes wait until the fight calms down.
- Remember the result per phone so the next battle starts at the right level.
- "Auto" is the default; Low/Medium/High stay as fixed manual choices.
- Targets: iPhone 13+ and Galaxy S23+ run smoothly; any Android must still play.
- Fix the known bug: a black wedge down the left edge on High at the Crown start.
- Numbers in `config.js` (`QUALITY`).

## How it works
- The ladder: `QUALITY.rungs`, best first. Going down: resolution 2 > 1.7 > 1.4, shadows High > Medium > off,
  clouds and far scenery off (sky clouds, water ripples, cloud-sea detail, birds), distant soldiers simpler
  (detailed radius 26 > 14 m, no outlines), then last-resort resolution 1 > 0.8. Each rung's `lv` sets what is
  built once at a battle's start (grass, crowd, particle caps), so those follow at the next battle.
- First guess: `guessRung` (chip families; iPhones name no chip so they start high and the 5 s probe sorts them;
  big screens one rung lower). Probe: the first 5 s may jump several rungs (one per 8 fps under 40).
- Steady watch: 3 s windows; under 40 fps one rung down; 55+ fps for 20 s one rung up; 10 s between changes;
  no climbing back to a rung it fell from within 60 s; a browser-held 30 fps (iPhone Low Power Mode) is left alone;
  stalls and hidden tabs void the window.
- Mid-fight (a swing or blow within 25 m of the camera's captain in the last 2 s): only resolution rungs move;
  a visible step waits and the resolution drops to x0.8 meanwhile. Joined phones adapt too.
- Kept: `localStorage['rally-quality-auto2']` = { chip + screen key, rung }. A different phone key starts from the guess.
- Code: rule `adaptStep` and guess `guessRung` in `render/quality.js` (pure); loop `watchdog` in `main.js`;
  live switches `applyPixelRatio`, `applyShadowQuality` (only when calm: it stalls a frame), `applyFar` in
  `render/scene.js`; cloud sea and birds read `quality.cfg.far` in `render/mountain.js`; soldier detail reads
  `cfg.near` / `cfg.outlines` in `render/soldiers.js`; the fight signal is `markFight` (soldiers.js, feel.js).
