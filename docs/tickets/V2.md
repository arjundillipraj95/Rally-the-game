# V2 Party voice: friends hear each other from the main screen, through the lobby, into the battle

Model: O · Written by the owner 14 Nov from the code and Arjun's agreed plan (UX first)
Status: built (not shipped), 14 Nov. `r_V2.mjs` passes 12/12; crown2sim/online pass roles off+on.

## What to build
Like a PUBG squad: once you're in a party with friends, you hear each other on the main screen, while
searching, in the lobby and in the battle, with no hang-up between them. Party = the Hub's party (max 3).

- Own voice link, separate from the battle link. Each phone in a party of 2+ opens a second PeerJS peer
  (random id) just for voice. Its id goes to the Hub (`{t:'voice', vp}`), and `partyUpdate` adds `vp` per
  member, so every phone knows its friends' voice ids. Every pair links once (lower id calls; the V1d
  call-back after 8 s and the retries carry over). Same relay list as the game (V1b).
- Persists: the battle starting or ending does not touch it. It closes only when you leave the party,
  the party drops to 1, or the app is closed. A friend joining: everyone links to them. A friend leaving: dropped.
- Mic: the first time you're in a party, a "Join voice" chip on the party bar (the ask needs a tap on
  phones). After that, it starts by itself in every party (remembered). Mic button: mute; one tap per friend to
  mute that friend (the party bar).
- Talking shown: a friend's name/portrait glows on the party bar while they speak (level from their audio).
- Battle: the same mic button and box as now. The V1 battle voice (calls to teammates through the battle link)
  is switched off: non-party teammates are not heard. Opt-in for them is V3.
- Diagnostics box: the V1d box moves to the voice link (ids, waiting / linked / via relay / hearing them).

## Done when (`store/r_V2.mjs`)
Three phones: A and B form a party on the main screen and hear each other (energy measured both ways) before any
search. C joins the party: all three pairs hear. They search Duo/Trio, the lobby and the battle start: no call
closes (same call objects, energy keeps arriving). C leaves the party: A and B still hear, C's link is gone.
A phone not in the party is never called. Plus `r_V1d.mjs` reworked for the voice link, crown2sim/online roles off+on.

## Design rules it relies on
None in the design doc for voice yet (Arjun's agreed plan, 14 Nov chat): reliability first, party voice core,
strangers opt-in later, no team/all switch until an all-players channel exists.

## Where to start in the code
- `online/src/index.js` `partyUpdate` (members list), `leaveParty`, a new `m_voice`.
- `game/src/net/online.js` `case 'party'` (ON.party), `send`.
- `game/src/net/voice.js` (all of it: becomes party-based; `teammates()` → party members with `vp`).
- `game/src/net/p2p.js` `peerOpts()` / `iceServers()` (reuse for the voice peer), `voice:` block (removed from battle use).
- `game/src/main.js` `partyOthers` and the party bar (chip, glow, per-friend mute), `stopVoice` at battle end (no longer stops party voice).

## Out of scope
Strangers on your team (V3, opt-in), all-players channel, push-to-talk, the team/all switch.

## Open questions
None if Arjun agrees with: mic starts by itself after the first "Join voice" tap; non-party teammates not heard until V3.
