## What to build
The Crown's ten minutes become one day, always the same (the standard; no time options):
| Match clock | Light |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Sunrise: sun just over the horizon, warm gold, long shadows, low mist in the valleys |
| 0:00-5:00 | Morning: the sun climbs, light whitens |
| 5:00 (war chest lands) | Noon: sun highest, brightest, shortest shadows |
| 5:00-7:30 | Afternoon: sun lowers, light warms to gold |
| 7:30 | Dusk (as today) |
| 8:00 | Night (as today: torches, drums, glacier) |
1. The sun actually moves across the sky (directional light angle, sky gradient, fog colour, F10b's
   cloud sea and distant peaks all follow it). Blend smoothly between keyframes; no visible steps.
   Keyframes (times, colours, sun angles, intensities) go in a `DAY` table in `game/src/config.js`.
   Today's dusk and night look must stay as it is at 7:30 and 8:00.
2. Herald: no new message types. Add time-of-day variants to the existing pools in
   `game/src/ui/feed.js` `HERALD`: sunrise lines to `start`, noon lines to `chestSoon` (and the chest's
   arrival line if it has a pool), sunset lines to the night-soon line. Keep the game's cheeky tone.
   Example only: "The sun's up. So are the arrows."
3. Retire match-length choices for the Crown: when the Crown is the mode, the 5 / 10 / 15 min
   selectors (home `index.html` ~L208 and lobby `#lobbyTime` ~L317) are hidden and the Crown always
   runs 10:00 (`MODES.crown.time` 600). Other modes keep their selectors. Check whether the Crown reads
   the selector today (`matchTime()` in `core/state.js` L85) and make sure it can't.
4. Online: every phone shows the same time of day (derive it from the match clock the host already
   shares; add nothing to snapshots if the clock is enough).
