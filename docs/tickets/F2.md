## What to build
STATE queue #9: one lobby for all 8 or 12 players: lanes (left, right, jungle), ready-up, 10 s versus screen.
Arjun's picks (14 Nov):
- One lobby for all the players of a matchmade battle (Duo 8, Trio 12; Solo's 4 the same way). Each player
  picks a lane: left, right or jungle. Then ready-up, then a 10 s versus screen showing every side before the
  battle loads.
- A player who leaves the lobby: a computer takes his seat right away. Anyone who joins later takes that seat
  from the computer.
- Works on phones in landscape (F15), online with the host as a player's phone, roles off and on.

## Gaps found in the code (14 Nov)
Service (`online/src/index.js`, Hub):
1. `room.sides[c]` is a packed list: when someone leaves (`cancelQueue` splices), everyone after him moves up a
   seat, so seats (and armies, `c + 4k`) shift under people. Seats need fixed slots (id or null = computer).
2. No lane and no ready state anywhere (no messages, nothing in `lobbyUpdate`).
3. Start rule is "every seat full, or 60 s (`LOBBY_WAIT`)". Nothing for ready-up.
4. A started room never frees a leaver's seat (`cancelQueue` only acts before start; `onClose`/`m_quit` keep him
   in `sides` and `seats`), so `fits()` counts him and a late joiner can never take his army, though the host's
   phone already hands it to the computer (`netHostReadInputs`) and would hand it on (`want` = that army).
5. The `start` message carries seats and nicks, not lanes or who is a computer.
Phones:
6. The lobby is the small search box on the home screen (`renderSearch` in main.js), floated over the warm-up
   fight; it shows names per army and "Open seat", no lane, no ready, no "Computer".
7. No versus screen: on `start` the host opens the battle (`matchHost`) and shows the private-battle lobby
   (`showLobby`, `ovLobby`) with "Linking up…"; joiners the same; the battle starts when all are linked or after
   25 s (`MATCH_WAIT`), and loads straight in.
8. Late joins (friend joins a running battle, searcher drops into one under 110 s) must skip lobby and versus.
9. The worker deploys from main: a new phone talking to the old live service must still work (no ready field =
   no ready button, no versus), and an old phone with the new service the same.
10. A matchmade battle's end screen: the host's "Back to lobby" opens the private lobby (`showLobby`). Not
    changed here (noted).
11. Lanes don't exist in the battle (AI or HUD). Design: "a plan only, not a rule", shown in the lobby only.

## Arjun's answers (14 Nov)
- Four armies (four teams) only, never two. Lanes are relative to your own home: Left and Right are your two lane
  castles as your own screen shows them at the start, Jungle is the village and field behind them. Versus = four columns.
- The lobby is a pop-up over the warm-up: pick a lane, tap Done (= ready), play the warm-up; a small bar (clock,
  seats filled, "Lobby") reopens it to change the lane; reopening doesn't cancel ready.
- Seat switch: tap a Computer seat in the pop-up to move there (any army). A party member moving to another army
  is asked "Are you sure you want to leave the party?"; yes = he leaves the party and moves, no = nothing changes.
  r_F2 checks both answers.
- Real-phone checks only after a ship (the service deploys from main): fine.
