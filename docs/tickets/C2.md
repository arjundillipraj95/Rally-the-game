# C2 Stone thrower (new unit)

Model: O · Written by the owner 18 Nov from the design doc (rev 159: Units table, "Health baselines", Orders, Gold, Devi card)
and plan P4.6, and the code on `launch26` (ff672e6)
Status: ready (waiting for Arjun's go). Needed before C4 (Devi recruits archers and stone throwers).

## What to build
A new soldier kind `thrower` (config: `STATS.thrower`, `THROWER_TIERS`; `UNITS.hp.thrower` 3 already exists).
- 300 health (15 infantry hits, 9 champion hits, 6 from behind); 5 places; 200 gold; slow.
- 40 m range, can't hit within 10 m; reload 6 s; a 2 s arcing stone with a ground marker; 3.5 m area, 45 at the centre to
  15 at the edge; x4 against gates (180 a stone: a castle gate 700 in 4, a small gate 450 in 3); 6 blows on the war chest;
  can't hit the glacier. Misses movers (it lands where they were), punishes standing lines and shield walls.
- Testudo: stones do half. Its own army follows its champion's stats (`champions/army.js` `unitRow` gets `thrower`).
- Orders: Follow = behind the army; Hold and Charge = fires at the biggest crowd or the nearest gate.
- Upgrades: **Fire pots** (120): every third shot burns the ground 5 s, reload 5.5 s. **Reinforced** (220): +50% health,
  50 m, faster. Fire on the ground is a new shared system (also Devi's fire arrows and Arrow storm): damage a second, a
  braced line inside it loses its brace.
- Recruit button lists it for champions whose page has `recruits` with `thrower`; bots field it.
- Joined phone: the unit, the stone's flight and marker, the burning ground.

## Done when
`store/r_C2.mjs`: a thrower kills nothing within 10 m; a stone on a standing 6-man line hits several; a runner is missed;
4 stones break a castle gate; fire pots burn on the 3rd shot; a testudo takes half; reinforced reaches 50 m. `r_C2-join`.
crown2sim, crown2online (roles off and on), r_PERF1c, lag no worse.

## Design rules it relies on
Units table "Stone thrower"; Health baselines (300%); Orders table; Gold table (200 / 120 / 220); "Stone throwers have no
cap"; "The glacier is captains only".

## Where to start in the code
`config.js` STATS / tiers / RECRUITS; `core/sim/soldier.js` (archer shooting branch as the model); `core/sim/missiles.js`
(a `stone` projectile with area damage, on the arrow pipeline but its own hit); `core/sim/army.js` recruit places;
`core/nav.js` gates; `champions/army.js` `unitRow`; `render/soldiers.js` (a new figure); `net/netgame.js` unit and
missile encoding.

## Build cost
Model (Claude in Blender): a small onager with an animated throwing arm, two crew on the shared soldier body, a stone and a
fire pot. Sounds: creak and thump on release, stone impact, fire whoosh and crackle loop.
