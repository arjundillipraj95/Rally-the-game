# F9 Horse charges

Model: O · Written by the builder 15 Nov from the code (Arjun's go: F9, F25, CL2)
Status: built (not shipped), on `t/F9`. r_F9 4/4 ok; crown2sim + crown2online ok, roles off and on

## What to build
A cavalry charge is fully stopped only by spearmen on Hold and facing it; standing men slow it; moving
or flanked men are bowled over. It looks and does the same thing (pose and knockback match the rule),
on the host and on joined phones.

## Done when
`game/store/r_F9.mjs`: a charging rider into (a) spearmen on Hold, still, facing: the charge stops, the
horse is hurt and the rider thrown off, the men barely move, they show the set-spear brace; (b) the
same men standing still but not on Hold (or not spearmen): the charge ends, the horse is slowed, the man
is shoved a little for little damage, not bowled; (c) men walking, or (d) still on Hold but hit from the
side: bowled over (full knock, full damage), the charge goes on.

## Design rules it relies on
Industry default (Total War / Unite): braced spears in front stop cavalry; non-braced standing infantry
absorb the charge's momentum; moving or flanked infantry get run down.

## Where to start in the code (gaps found)
- `core/sim.js` rider loop (~L2779): three branches.
  1. "Spear wall!" stops ANY rider on any still tier-1+ footman facing him (no Hold needed): gap.
  2. "The line holds!" uses `canBrace` (L177): stops a charge on any holding man OR any man walking at
     0.3-3.5 m/s, any kind: gap (moving men must be bowled, only spearmen on Hold stop it).
  3. Bowled over: everyone else. No "standing men slow it" case at all: gap.
- `render/heroes.js chargeAt` uses `canBrace`: walking men show a brace and are then bowled: gap.
- The set-spear pose for footmen isn't sent to joined phones (`holding`/order not in the snapshot): gap.
  `u.bracing` (bit 16384) is sent and drawn (soldiers.js planted spear, heroes.js POSES.brace).
- A joined rider predicts his own charge; when the host ends it the phone keeps galloping: gap.

## Plan

Joined rider: a kick with stun while charging ends his charge on his phone.
