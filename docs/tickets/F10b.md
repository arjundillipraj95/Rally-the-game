# F10b The summit feeling (redo of the F10 edge)

Model: O · Written by the owner 2026-11-11 · Builds on F10 (ae139a3, on roles, not shipped)
Status: built on `f10b` (not shipped); Arjun judges the feel on his phone

## What to build
Arjun played F10: "not that great; it should feel like users are really on top of a mountain."
F10's ring of tall needle peaks stands right at the edge like a fence, so players look UP at a wall.
On a summit you look DOWN and OUT. Agreed direction (Arjun's go, 11 Nov):
1. Remove F10's needle ring. Past the playable rim the ground breaks into steep rock cliffs that fall
   away below the player (a drop, not a wall).
2. A sea of clouds below rim level, out to the horizon, drifting slowly (one large plane or ring with
   a soft cloud shader/texture; brighter where the sun hits).
3. Distant summits rising out of the clouds: a few broad snowy massifs, far away, mostly at or below
   the player's height. No spikes.
4. Sky and air: deeper blue overhead, pale haze at the horizon, a visible bright sun; a few birds
   circling below the rim; light wind with snow blown near the rim. Dusk/night must still work
   (clouds and range take the fog/night colours).
5. Camera unchanged. Budget: at most ~5 extra draw calls and +5% triangles at the start of a Crown
   battle on Low versus commit 4cafda1 (before F10); measure both.
