# F10 / M2 The mountain's edge

Model: S · Written by the owner 2026-11-11 from design rev 40, plan rev 19
Status: built on branch f10 (not shipped), see STATE

## What to build
Friends' playtest F10: "the map's edge is a slope". Plan M2: ring the summit with taller rocky peaks
and cliffs falling into mist, and a distant range against the sky, so the map reads as a summit among
mountains instead of a green edge that drops away. Camera unchanged; no floating island.
Ships live like Phase 0.

## Done when
Screenshots from three edges (low quality and high), plus `crown2sim.mjs` and `crown2online.mjs`
passing. Draw calls and triangles at the start of a Crown battle on Low must not go up by more than
about 5% (F1 says they are already far over budget: 1,219 draw calls, 560k triangles). Print both
numbers before and after in the report.
