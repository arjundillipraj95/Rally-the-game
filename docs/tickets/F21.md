# F21 Music that follows you (recorded orchestra)

Model: O · Written 14 Nov from Arjun's sounds chat (his "go" after the sound shortlist page)
Status: built (not shipped)

## What to build
Keep the score engine (three levels, bar-line changes, stingers) and play recorded orchestra notes in place of
synth tones, so the music sits low under the battle and follows the player:
- Calm, no enemy near: a soft string pad and the odd harp note, no drums.
- On the move: a hand drum keeps your pace and the tempo lifts a little.
- Approach and fight: plucked bass, war drums (bass drum and timpani), hand-drum fills, horn calls.
- Low health (under 35%): the music muffles and drifts back, a heartbeat comes up, faster near death. The
  heartbeat plays with the music switched off too.
- Night on the Crown: drums held low, darker strings.
- Gong under "captain slain" and "night".
Synthesis stays as the fallback until the samples load. Arjun's aims: the player feels he is there, humour is subtle.
