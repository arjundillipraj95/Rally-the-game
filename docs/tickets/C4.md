## What to build
Devi's page `src/champions/devi.js` (under 300 lines) on the C1 framework and RANGE1.
- **C4a Page, stats, army.** Health 300; arrows of 24 at 1.75/s (42 a second); range 14 m; 5.9 / 11.2 m/s. Chola bow;
  `ranged` (RANGE1): homing, sticky, third shot two of 18, plant 0.2 s. Southern war horse (rules as Titus). Recruits
  archers and stone throwers. Her soldiers follow her stats (-25% health, -20% damage, +25% rate, -5% speed, range share
  0). Budget check passes (hp -25%, dps 0, foot -4.8%, ranged +30%). `?botchamp=devi`; in CHAMP_IDS, lobby, Champion setting.
- **C4b Piercing shot (level 3, 7 s).** Drag aims, an 18 m line shows first; 0.25 s draw; through everything in the line:
  50 to champions, 30 to soldiers, all slowed 30% for 1.5 s; a galloping horse hit stops dead 0.5 s (rider stays on);
  a front block stops it. From the saddle she stays on (Arjun 18 Nov). +5% a level.
- **C4c Evade (level 5, 8 s).** 5 m dash in 0.2 s the way you drag (tap: away from the nearest enemy); untouchable during
  it (`iframeT`); stops at walls.
- **C4d Tiger's volley (level 7, ult, 60 s).** Rooted 2.5 s: 20 arrows of 8 down an 18 m line (160 if all land), each hits
  the first unit in line; she turns up to 90 deg a second; a stun or knock-up ends it; a raised front shield takes each arrow at 30% (Arjun 18 Nov).
- **C4e Arrow storm (level 7, army ult, from the full horn).** Every archer of hers fires at one 10 m area for 4 s; a 2 s
  warning marker for everyone; Agni Villalar's fire arrows set the ground burning (C2's fire); her stone throwers fire too.
  A testudo takes nothing from its arrows, only the stones.
- **C4f Her archer line.** Villalar, then Agni Villalar (fire arrows, x3 against gates, towers and throwers), then Vil
  Veeran (longbow 26 m, +20% damage; a galloping horse 1 in 2). Today's ARCH_TIERS are one generic line: becomes a line
  per champion. Tier looks: leather caps, simple bows, plain versions of her. Stone throwers: fire pots, then reinforced.
- **C4g Duels.** She slings the bow on her back (0.5 s flourish) and fights with two arrows held like daggers at her own
  quick rhythm, under the shared duel rules (base 400 / 42, Attack, Heavy, Block, Evade, parry, Execute); puts them back
  when it ends. Her own execute.
- **C4h Lines.** Her 10 lines and her army's (new moments: Arrow storm, stone throwers) on her page, speech bubbles (C3j).
- **C4i Art in.** Her body from the turnaround sheet on the shared skeleton (`tools/models/titus/build_body.py`), bow,

## Design rules it relies on
Devi card (18 Nov); rulebook: one job per ability, area shown first, wind-up 0.3 s or less, never locked over 0.5 s
(ults aside), no stun over 1 s; a full combo at most 50% of a champion in 2 s (her worst: Piercing + volley 2 s = 178,
44%); duels: every ranged champion has a melee sidearm.
