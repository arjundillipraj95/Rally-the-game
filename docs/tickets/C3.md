# C3 Titus, Roman Vanguard

## What to build
Titus's page (`src/champions/titus.js`, under 300 lines) on the C1 framework, each part its own card, commit and go.
- **C3a Page, stats, combo, army.** Health 480; 30 damage a hit at 1.2 hits a second (36 a second: slow and heavy);
  reach 1.4 m (shield arm); 5.9 m/s on foot, 11.2 riding. Gladius and scutum only. Built on C1g (page in shown numbers, rate, reach, limits).
  Combo: each hit 30, at his rate.
  Combo: slash, slash, shield bash (staggers: today's bash, BASH). Mount: Roman battle horse (horse, as today); riding fast
  he strikes with today's ride-by, slow or standing basic only. Recruits spearmen and archers. His soldiers (Arjun 17 Nov):
  neutral soldiers are the baseline; his men take every stat by his own share off the base champion (+20% health, -14%
  hit rate, -5% pace, +8% reach). Shield discipline: designer to decide. Bots can play him (?botchamp=titus).
- **C3b Ram of Cassius (level 3, 6 s).** 5 m shield-first rush; low damage to every unit touched; soldiers shoved aside and
  stumble (nobody flies); the first champion hit knocked back 2 m and stunned 0.5 s. Stops at walls and gates.
- **C3c Shield of Cassius (level 5, 10 s after it ends).** 4 s: blocks everything from the front, arrows too, also for allies
  behind him; he moves at 70% and can back away; hits from behind still land; doesn't stop army ults.
- **C3d Eagle's fall (level 7, ult).** Leap up to 10 m onto a unit (tap: the nearest champion; drag aiming comes with the
  battle screen, S1-15); landing: 25% of a champion's health within 4 m, soldiers knocked down 1 s; lands shield up, allies
  within 4 m take 20% less for 4 s. Nothing interrupts the leap; a target that dies mid-leap: he lands on the spot. From the
  saddle it is a leap off the horse.
- **C3e Testudo march (level 7, army ult, from the full horn).** Shields lock over his army, everyone heals 25%; the block
  marches about 10 m over 4 s to the marked point to a war drum. Facing forward: flanks and back open; arrows do nothing,
  stones half, fire pots land, basic attacks can't break it, a horse charge into it stalls.
- **C3f Titus the default.** You and the bots play Titus unless chosen otherwise (legionary stays for tests and comparison).

## Design rules it relies on
Titus card (design doc, Champions); rulebook: one job per ability, area shown first, wind-up 0.3 s or less, never locked
over 0.5 s (ults aside), no stun over 1 s, animation decides the look and code the effect. Edge cases: his shield doesn't stop
army ults; the Ram stops at walls and gates; Eagle's fall lands on the marked spot if the target dies; nothing interrupts it.
