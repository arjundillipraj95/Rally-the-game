## What changed
F17c drew the spear combo's second blow as a sweep while the rules still struck a thrust (1.3x, +1.1 m reach, through to
the man behind). Now the rules strike it like a sword swing: `CC.spear.sweep` = 1.0x damage, his own reach (no spear
extra), the man in front only, a light knock (.25), a half-step in (2.2), aimed at 3.6 m; recovery `cd2` (.7 s) as before.
Poke (1st) and skewer lunge (3rd) unchanged. Code: `core/sim.js` the spear branch of a press (`sweep`), `config.js`.
The Defender's Spear sweep ability (Phase 1) stays the big one.
