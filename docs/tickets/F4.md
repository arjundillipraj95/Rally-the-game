# F4 Neutral garrisons and camps fight as real soldiers

Model: O · Written by the owner 2026-11-14 from the code on `roles` (157cc9b), design rev 40, plan rev 19
Status: built (not shipped), on `t/F4` (Arjun's go 14 Nov)

## What to build
Friends' playtest, 10 Nov: today a castle's garrison or a jungle camp is one shared health pool (a `c2` "break" of
kind `guard`/`camp`) with five painted figures standing still. Make them real soldiers:
- Each man fights and dies on his own (own hp, own blows, own death fall), same rules as any army's soldier.
- They hold their post: stand in formation at it, facing out, until roused.
- Ambush: an army's man (or captain) passing within the ambush radius, or a blow/arrow on one of them, rouses the
  whole post. Footmen go for the nearest foe, archers shoot from the post. They never chase past the leash; with no
  foe inside it for a short calm, they walk back, heal to full at the post and stand guard again (Unite camp reset).
- Taking a camp or hold means beating its men: a castle can't be taken while one guard lives; the last camp man
  cut down pays the camp's gold to the killer's army, and the camp's men come back after `back` seconds.
- Numbers in config.js tables. Smooth on low phones (draw calls checked against F14's Auto rungs). Same on every phone online.

## Gaps found in the code (all to close)
Sim (`game/src/core/sim.js`)
1. `c2Begin` (1608) builds one `breaks` entry per post with a pooled `hp`; no units exist. Needs: real units per
   post (3 foot, 2 arch) with fixed ids, plus the post kept as a record (spot, kind, its men, awake, back timer).
2. The post's fighting is faked in `update`'s break loop (1759-1786): `b.cd` swings hit every man in reach at once
   (one footman's blow each, no swing of their own), archers fire from the break. Replace with each man's own
   swing/shoot through the normal code (`startSwing`, `resolveSwing`, `shoot`).
3. `c2Hit` (1631) / `c2BreakInReach` (1627) / `towerInReach` (1558): captains and footmen hit the post's pole, with
   a 1.5x captain bonus. Posts must no longer be hittable; their men are hit like any enemy.
4. `c2Garrison` (1622) works out men standing from the pool; used by render/mountain.js. Becomes a count of live men.
5. `c2Guard` (1567) holds a castle while the pool lives; becomes "any man of that garrison alive". `c2Hold` (1568)
   counts every unit in the hold by `allianceOf` (colorOf), so a neutral man inside would count for side 0: skip neutrals.
6. Camp reset (1761, `b.back`) refills the pool; becomes respawning the five men at the post.
7. `K.clearT` (1787) "every castle's garrison down": from live men.
8. `mkUnit` (24) reads `G.teams[ti]` (footTier, horseMax): crashes for a neutral ti. Needs its own maker.
9. Neutral ti: figures use `ti = ARMIES` (12); `colorOf(12) = 0`, so `isEnemy`/`isEnemyTi` (state.js 89-90) and
   `allianceOf` would make them side 0's friends. Needs: neutral is everyone's enemy, not its own.
10. `update` unit loops (≈2620-2680) index per-army arrays and `G.teams[u.ti]` (meleeN, aura, tkey, slot,
   thinkSoldier, plan walls): neutrals must take their own branch (`thinkNeutral`) and skip army bookkeeping.
11. `die` (1163): `G.teams[killer.ti].gold += 8`, tallies, morale (`loseHeart(u.ti)`), cap-kill points: guard for
   a neutral victim (no morale, no results tally, per-man gold from config) and a neutral killer (cause 'garrison',
   no killer credit). Last man of a post: camp gold + highlight to the killer's army (as `c2Hit` did).
12. Arrows (`shoot`, `arrowsTick` 2827-2847, hit at 1160): the `neutral` flag is set by hand on break arrows;
   set it from a neutral shooter. Kills by them read 'garrison' on the death card.
13. `shieldTick` (448), `nearestFoe` (386), duels/`spared`: work once isEnemy is right; check neutrals never join
   duel rings or execution guarding.
14. `planGoal0` (2225) sends computer captains to the guard break's spot: keep the post spot; they then fight the men.

## Done when (`game/store/r_F4.mjs`)
a. Crown start: a post per castle and camp (20: 12 castles, 8 camps) x 5 = 100 neutral men in `G.units`, at their spots, grey, asleep.
b. One footman struck: only his hp drops; cut down, he falls on his own and the post fights on with 4.
c. Ambush: an enemy man walks within the ambush radius: the post wakes within 1 s and attacks; he runs off: no man
   goes past the leash; after the calm they're back at their spots at full hp, asleep.
d. Castle: not takeable while one guard lives; takeable once all five are down. Camp: last kill pays `gold` to the
   killer's army; five men back at the post after `back` s.
e. Joined phone: same alive men per post as the host, awake men within 1 m, asleep men on their spots.
f. Cost: draw calls at the Crown start on High and Low (the levels of F14's top and bottom rungs) within +2 of before;
   snapshot bytes with all posts asleep within +5%; whole-battle sim under 2.5 ms a step; 100 sleeping men under
   0.3 ms a step (measured with and without them, armies kept home). (The first card said +15% sim time: a sleeping
   post costs ~0.1-0.2 ms on a 0.2 ms base, under 1% of a frame; a garrison that fights costs what fighting soldiers cost.)
Plus crown2sim and crown2online, roles off and on.
