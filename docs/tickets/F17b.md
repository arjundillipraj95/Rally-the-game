# F17b Strikes go where he faces: the spear points ahead, the swoosh is traced by the real blade (on foot and mounted), and the water stops looking like paper

Model: Opus · Written and built 2026-11-13 in Arjun's animation chat (his bug report with phone screenshots, then "deploy all together to roles")
Status: built, tests below. On `roles`.

## The bugs (Arjun, from his phone)
1. "The strike is going somewhere else while the hit/the swoosh is going elsewhere." Spear thrust: "he is looking straight and hitting sideways."
2. The lake "looks like a paper cover".
3. Same as 1 on horseback.

## What was wrong
- Spear: the F17 thrust clip was made for a sword (the blade points ahead from a bent wrist). F17 laid the shaft along
  the forearm, which in that clip angles across the body: measured in the game, the spear pointed ~60 degrees off his
  facing for the whole thrust (55 at rest). The rules land the hit in a narrow cone straight ahead (core/sim.js).
- Swoosh: the captain is drawn on the clip skeleton, but the trail and the crescent swoosh were still placed from the
  old code arm (`M.wArm` in render/soldiers.js) and the body centre with a fixed arc: nothing to do with the blade you see.
- Water: weak swell normals (.045), a flat colour and a regular grid of sparkle dots: a printed sheet from above.

## What was built
1. `render/rig/cliprig.js`: keeps the spear's pivot (`R.shaft`, its first hold in `R.shaftQ0`) and marks the weapon's
   point and where its blade starts (`R.blade.tip`, `R.blade.base`), measured from the weapon's own bounds.
2. `render/heroes.js` `aimShaft`: each frame a spear or javelin points at a spot straight ahead of his facing
   (`ANIM.shaftAim`: 3 m out, chest height; mounted 4.5 m and dipped a touch, a couched lance). The grip stays in the
   fist; a code pose blended in keeps its own hold. Measured: from ~60 degrees off to 3-14 (the rest is his hand being
   out to the side, angling in to the spot the hit lands). `heroBlade(u, base, tip)`: the blade's world points.
3. `render/effects.js` `bladeTrail`: a ribbon from the blade's base to its tip, sampled each frame of the swing,
   smoothed (Catmull-Rom), bright at the point, fading over .26 s. 10 reused. Drawn over everything, like the old swoosh.
4. `render/soldiers.js`: a captain or soldier drawn on the clip skeleton gets the ribbon from his real blade (near the
   camera); his fixed crescents are skipped. The spear's straight streak stays (it now matches the aimed spear). The far,
   code-drawn figures keep the old crescents.
5. `render/water.js`: swells in three directions at uneven frequencies, stronger (.11) with ripples (.055 High, .025
   Low), deeper colour in broad slow patches, tight sun glints (shininess 260) instead of the dot grid, and a light
   fresnel (the sky shows at a glancing look, it clears looking down).

Checked: the slash's blade crosses the front at the moment of the hit (18 degrees off his facing, 1.7 m out; mounted 5
degrees), pictures on foot and mounted; lake and river pictures, Low and High.
