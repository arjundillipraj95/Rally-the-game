## Arjun's picks (13 Nov)
1. **Captain respawn and game start, one sequence**: 0.4 s fade to black, the camera moves while black, 0.4 s fade in
   as he marches out of his gate with a dust puff and a horn; he can't be hit for 1 s.
   **Soldiers**: the same, smaller.
2. **Duel push**: shoved to the ring's edge over 0.3 s, fast then slowing, a stumble and dust; then the ring rises.
   Quick and natural.
3. **Execution**: the executioner takes two quick steps (0.25 s) into place, facing the victim; 0.15 s slow motion on
   the hit.
4. **Chasm fall, "300" style**: 0.5 s slow motion at the edge, flail and comic scream, fall out of sight, counted dead
   after about 1.2 s. Your own captain: the camera holds at the edge looking down, then the respawn fade.
5. **Check and smooth**: battle start after the flyover; battle end to the results screen.
6. Pushed out of the glacier's footprint: kept as is (Arjun, 12 Nov).
