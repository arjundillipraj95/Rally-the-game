# Rally! — store listing (CrazyGames)

## Title
Rally!

## Short description (one line)
Lead your squad into loud, silly, shield-smashing battles: four armies, one castle each, no mercy for helmets.

## Description
Rally! drops you into the helmet of a captain with a squad at your back and three rival armies on the field.

Swing a sword through a three-hit combo, switch to a spear to skewer two men at once, hurl javelins, or jump and slam down into a crowd and send them flying (screaming, usually). Recruit footmen and archers, buy upgrades, call your horse, and bark orders: Follow, Hold, Charge, or lock shields into a Shieldwall while the arrows bounce off.

Four ways to fight:
- **Conquest** – tear down every enemy castle.
- **Deathmatch** – the last army with tickets wins.
- **Capture the Fort** – carry the banner home three times.
- **Control** – hold the lettered points and build your score.

Nine battlefields, from a Roman forum to a snowy hill, three difficulty levels, team-ups (2v2, 3v1…), ranks and helmet crests to earn, and chickens who really didn't sign up for this.

Play alone against the computer, or share a four-letter code and fight with friends on their own phones — no accounts needed.

## Controls
**Mobile:** drag on the left to move, drag on the right to turn the camera. Big red button attacks (hold to keep swinging), shield button blocks, arrows button jumps, swap button changes weapon. The bar along the bottom recruits, upgrades, gives orders, calls a volley and your horse.

**Keyboard:** WASD move · arrows turn · Space attack · Shift block · C jump · R switch weapon · H horse · V volley · Q follow · F hold · E charge · T shieldwall · 1/2 recruit · 3–8 upgrades · U upgrade menu.
