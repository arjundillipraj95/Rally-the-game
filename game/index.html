Rally!

Lead your army against three rival teams, alone or with friends on their own phones. Recruit footmen and archers, call your horse, and fight at the front of your squad.

Turn your phone sideways for the best view
How to play
Move
Left thumb. Drag the right side to turn the camera.
Fight
Red attacks (hold to keep swinging). Blue blocks hits from the front.
Horse
Ride to cross the map and trample men on foot. No blocking, no castle damage, no banner. Spearmen are deadly to horses.
Units
Footmen upgrade into spear & javelin fighters who dismount riders. Archers deal more damage on a headshot, less on the legs; shields block body shots but not headshots.
Teams
Allies share victory and never hurt each other. In Capture the Fort, allies pool their captures.
Orders
Follow me, Hold here, Charge, and Shieldwall: your squad locks into a tight line ahead of you and marches slowly while arrows from the front bounce off.
Aura
Soldiers inside the ring around your captain hit harder, block more and move faster. Upgrades make it bigger.
Keys
WASD, arrows turn, Space attack, Shift block, H ride, Q follow, F hold, E charge, T shieldwall, 1/2 recruit, 3–8 upgrades.

Play withfriends

Up to eight players across four castles — take a color solo, or toggle Duo in the lobby to share a castle with a friend. Empty seats are played by the computer.

No accounts needed. The host gets a four-letter code; friends open this page on their phones and type it in.
