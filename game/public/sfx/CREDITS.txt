Sound effects in this folder are CC0 (public domain) recordings, trimmed, normalised and re-encoded:

- Kenney "Impact Sounds" and "RPG Audio" (CC0) - https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds , https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
    clang*, heavy*, wall*, thud*, fall*, step*, cloth*, coins*
- qubodup "37 Hits/Punches" (CC0) - https://opengameart.org/content/37-hitspunches
    hit*
- Ogrebane "Battle Sound Effects" (offered under CC0 among others) - https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects
    swing1-3, bow1
- artisticdude "RPG Sound Pack" (CC0) - https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
    swing4-6, draw*

mus/*.mp3: single notes and hits from Versilian Studios "VSCO 2 Community Edition" (CC0) -
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE (horn, cello and viola sections, harp, cello pizzicato, timpani,
bass drum, frame drum, conga, gong). Trimmed, mono, levelled. The music engine in src/ui/audio.js plays them.

The battle-start horns in sfx.horn and the distant battle bed are still synthesised in src/ui/audio.js
(with the music off, the war drums are too).

scream1-6 are original: synthesised for Rally by scripts/screams.py (a small source-filter voice model).
