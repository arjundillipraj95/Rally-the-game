Sound effects in this folder are CC0 (public domain) recordings, trimmed, normalised and re-encoded:

- Kenney "Impact Sounds" and "RPG Audio" (CC0) - https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds , https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
    clang*, heavy*, wall*, thud*, fall*, step*, cloth*, coins*
- qubodup "37 Hits/Punches" (CC0) - https://opengameart.org/content/37-hitspunches
    hit*
- Ogrebane "Battle Sound Effects" (offered under CC0 among others) - https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects
    swing1-3, bow1
- artisticdude "RPG Sound Pack" (CC0) - https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
    swing4-6, draw*

War drums, horns and the distant battle bed are synthesised in src/ui/audio.js.
