Rally!

Lead your army against three rival teams, alone or with friends on their own phones. Recruit footmen, spearmen and archers, call your horse, and fight at the front of your squad.

Turn your phone sideways for the best view
How to play
Move
Left thumb. Drag the right side to turn the camera.
Fight
Red attacks (hold to keep swinging). Blue blocks hits from the front.
Horse
Ride to cross the map and trample men on foot. No blocking, no castle damage, no banner. Spearmen are deadly to horses.
Units
Footmen beat archers. Spearmen beat footmen and riders. Archers beat spearmen.
Teams
Allies share victory and never hurt each other. In Capture the Fort, allies pool their captures.
Keys
WASD, arrows turn, Space attack, Shift block, H ride, F orders, 1/2/3 recruit.

Play withfriends

Up to four players, each leading their own color. Empty colors are played by the computer.

No accounts needed. The host gets a four-letter code; friends open this page on their phones and type it in.
